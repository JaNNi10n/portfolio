# Product: Shivansh Bhardwaj portfolio

- **Register:** brand (the portfolio is the product).
- **Users:** (1) recruiters at Bangalore product companies and startups, screening many candidates fast, often on mobile, looking for role, location, availability and a resume; (2) AI/ML hiring engineers who click through to results files and READMEs to check that the numbers are real.
- **Purpose:** get Shivansh shortlisted for entry-level AI/ML Engineer roles. Success: within the first screen and a half a visitor sees who he is, the role, one real proof, the headline results and the resume link, and can check any result against its source file.
- **Personality:** calibrated, candid, instrumented. Emotional goal: trust.
- **Anti-references:** neural-network particle backgrounds, cyberpunk neon, AI-purple gradients, skill bars, image-only project grids, 3D or canvas navigation, preloaders and counters, navy/teal developer-portfolio clones, a chat agent as the only way in.
- **Principles:**
  1. Show the file, not the claim.
  2. Print limits next to results.
  3. The first screen answers who, what role, one proof and how to reach him.
  4. Motion never delays or hides content.
  5. Entry-level honest: no inflated numbers, no invented outcomes.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA minimum, AAA target for body text, full keyboard support, reduced motion respected, no colour-only meaning.
